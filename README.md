`main.py` writes a single first-person childhood memory to `/output/entry.txt`:
an August evening in grandmother's kitchen — banana fritters on her blue
kerosene stove, seven geckos counted on the wall — and the late realization,
years later, that she saved the crispiest fritter for the child. One scene,
one named person, one warm ache in the final clause.

Run: `python3 main.py` — writes `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, within the 280-character gate).
